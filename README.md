BrightTV Analysis is a focused evaluation of user behaviour and viewing patterns that aims to uncover key trends, consumption drivers, and opportunities for growth.


📊 BrightTV Viewership Analytics


📌 Overview

This project analyzes user behavior and content consumption patterns for BrightTV, a streaming platform aiming to grow its subscription base. Using user profile data and viewing session records, the analysis focuses on identifying trends, understanding drivers of engagement, and providing actionable recommendations to support Customer Value Management (CVM) strategies.


🔍 Key Insights

Viewership is concentrated during specific hours (typically evenings), indicating strong prime-time engagement behavior.
Certain content categories drive significantly higher engagement, suggesting strong audience preference trends.
Some days show consistently lower activity, presenting opportunities for targeted interventions.
Age and possibly gender segments show variation in viewing duration and content preferences.


💡 Recommendations
Supersport & Cricket together drive 31% of all views and over 40% of total watch time. Sports must remain a cornerstone of the content strategy.
47% of viewers are 25–34 and 32% are based in Gauteng. Targeted digital campaigns in urban hubs can maximise ROI.
Channel O and Trace TV collectively hold 2,000+ views, ranking 3rd–4th. Investing in Afro-centric music content may deepen audience loyalty.
Viewership grew 119% from January to March — suggesting strong seasonal or content-driven momentum heading into Q2.
